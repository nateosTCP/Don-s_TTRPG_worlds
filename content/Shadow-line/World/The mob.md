There are big 3 crime families. The flasks, The 11th number and the voodoo syndicate.

It runs the families mostly contain themselves in a different city but there is often conflict between them. The flasks and the voodoo syndicate often but heads, one of their brawls end with flask’s wife and kids getting killed, which only made the Violence between the two families increase.

#### [[The flasks]].
Run by [[Albert flask]], who 5 years lost his family in fight with the voodoo syndicate. They are most well-known for the racketeering a large number of government officials under their thumb.

#### [[The 11th number]].
Run by [[Don]] and [[Capy]], they are relatively mysterious and not a lot is really known about them. They keep themselves out of the spotlight and as such tend to have less open conflict.

#### [[The voodoo syndicate]].
Has been without a true head for 3 years now, on an ever-changing hierarchy. Whoever is must fit to lead will do so. They are most well known for their drug Oftentimes experimentally heavily making new drugs. Sometimes the new ones are relatively harmless Other times though they produce highly addictive substances that made people go mad.

## Lesser crime families.
