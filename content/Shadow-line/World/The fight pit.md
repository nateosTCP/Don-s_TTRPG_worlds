  The only place you can bet your hard-earned gold on fights to the death. Do to our advance  technology and rituals when ever a fighter dies they come back allowing for brutal fights and amazing spectacles!

The fight pit itself is located outside of town in an old warehouse. Its kept in relatively good condition Although it is falling apart in places due to the rowdiness of the crowds.

There is a betting counter where you can place bets on how the fight will go. At this counter is also the list of all the fights happening the prize money for the fighters and the information if you wish to join one of these fights yourself.

In the very center of the warehouse there is a walled off pit. The walls preventing people from falling in are made of the arcane, An ethereal energy that almost feels cold to the touch as if the walls were made of iron.
The pit looks big enough to house a 6V6 fights but it also looks like it could be shrunk to handle any number of combatants.

On the walls of the warehouse, you can find posters for both fighters and for bands, it seems like this place is sometimes used as a Concert Hall or club.
