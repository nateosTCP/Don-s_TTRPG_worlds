# tagline
A goblin who throws pigeons and rats at people and eats out of the garbage.
# backstory
worked at a dog food manufacturer before it got shut down. Unable to find another job one of his old buddies offered him a room for very cheap on top of Caddy's pub.

Some of his outfit is costumes he found thrown out behind a theatre Very often leading to Quite frankly ridiculous costumes.

main outfit: costume jewelry and his coat. he has a crown with a lot of missing glass jewels.

He’s got very patched up pants and dress shirt and an old, grey tweed waist coat with a prop pocket watch that doesn’t actually tell the time and his shoe soles are barely hanging on. They’re glued on with hopes and dreams.

His coat is made of cheap red velvet with a clumpy, tangled fur collar and some gold embellishments and embroidery. He’s got a bunch of costume rings on, some missing a few stones. He also has some costume necklaces as well as chains and pendants.

If someone points it out he just says “oh these? I got em in the war” “what war? Oh THE war” and he never elaborates past that
